    How Solkatt works
    Phaseopening
    Question in phase—

    Solkatt is a rule-based reflection coach. Mirror revisits your early thinking around the problem and the solution you imagined. Challenger questions your value proposition — the need, the benefit, and the competition (NABC, Carlson & Wilmot, SRI International). Coach helps you regulate and refine your thinking around Need, Approach, Benefit and Competition, so your idea can grow into a genuine entrepreneurial opportunity (opportunity recognition: Shane & Venkataraman, 2000).

    Phase design

    • • Mirror: the problem you saw, the solution you imagined, and where the idea came from
    • • Challenger: questioning need, benefit per cost, and competition — the NABC ingredients of your value proposition
    • • Coach: NABC self-regulation — test the need, refine the approach, state the benefit, evaluate against alternatives
    • • One question at a time; depth feedback, no generic praise; one re-scaffolding retry
    • • Final summary built only from your own words
